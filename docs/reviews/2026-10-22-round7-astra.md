## New findings

Both findings concern the newly introduced turbine mixture path. R1 is a newly exposed numerical regression; R2 describes the physically incomplete replacement for the prior turbine treatment. ## R1 — Partial-pressure turbine calls silently flatten the property table

- **severity:** medium
- **area:** correctness
- **file:line:** crates/bwr-core/src/bop.rs:589
- **wrong:** The new call multiplies both turbine pressures by `y_water` without checking the property domain. `steam.rs:87` clamps every saturation lookup below 657.088 Pa to the same 1 C row; `entropy_hp` and `isentropic_drop` then lose the low-pressure vapour entropy dependence. Isolated coupled fixture: sealed 40 C vessel, water mass `V_VESSEL_FLUID * rho_f(40 C) * (1 - 1e-5)`, 0.027793936 kg air giving 1 MPa total pressure, bypass closed, MSIVs/TCV initially open and turbine reset. The accepted dt=0.05 s step delivers 0.002579259 kg/s water and 0.555878723 kg/s air, with chest/condenser total pressures 7357.540/5928.623 Pa. Water partial pressures are 54.331214/43.779462 Pa. `work_limit` and shaft power are exactly zero, with no model error. Independent IF97 Region 2 at the same water enthalpy, 2573.542 kJ/kg, gives inlet/outlet temperatures 38.715911/22.497518 C and an isentropic drop of 30264.607 J/kg, or 25724.916 J/kg after the model's 0.85 factor. Both states are superheated vapour above 0 C; no ice or subzero extrapolation is needed. The reference uses the published [IAPWS-IF97 Region 2 formulation](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IF97-Rev), evaluated independently in `if97.py`.
- **consequence:** Supported gas-bearing admission can silently produce zero work under a positive expansion ratio. The simpler standalone limit `h=2573542 J/kg`, `y_water=0.01`, total outlet=6000 Pa reports zero available work for total pressure ratios 1.01, 1.1, 2 and 10 because both partial pressures map to the same row. This is an algebraic property error, not an integration error. The coupled fixture is initially two-phase and supplies only dome gas; later L8 protection does not repair the accepted step's property calculation.
- **fix:** Evaluate low-pressure superheated vapour with a valid pressure-dependent formulation, such as IF97 Region 2, and select phases from a consistent state solver. Where the chosen model cannot represent a state, reject or isolate that turbine admission explicitly rather than returning clamped properties. Add an independent all-vapour expansion check below the saturation-table pressure floor and a coupled accepted-flow regression. Do not merely lower the pressure argument to another arbitrary floor.

## R2 — Frozen vapour composition is inconsistent with wet mixture expansion

- **severity:** medium
- **area:** physics
- **file:line:** crates/bwr-core/src/bop.rs:581
- **wrong:** The new model preserves inlet water mole fraction through the turbine while calling a pure-water isentrope that condenses water. Conservation of total water/air mass does not preserve the gas-phase mole fraction when liquid forms. In the 90 C/180 t/100 kg air fixture, inlet water mole fraction is 0.30519847 and air/water mass ratio is 3.615915. At condenser total pressure 5928.62 Pa, the code prescribes water partial pressure 1809.40576 Pa. Independent IF97 reproduces the helper's wet endpoint: approximately 15.91934 C and water quality 0.849679. With that condensed fraction and the same retained air, the gas-phase water mole fraction would instead be approximately 0.274211 under ideal mixing, already inconsistent with the imposed 0.305198. In addition, the water-only isentrope and zero air work omit the common-temperature mixture entropy/enthalpy balance. An independent equilibrium ideal-air/IF97-water calculation at the same inlet and total outlet pressure gives 8.68419 C, remaining vapour fraction 0.526129 of the initial water, and outlet water mole fraction 0.189591. These are limiting-model calculations, not plant reference data; they use an ideal-air approximation and neglect liquid pressure corrections. The relevant phase/composition thermodynamics are covered by [IAPWS's humid-air formulation](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/SeaAir).
- **consequence:** Correcting inlet partial pressure removes the earlier invented inlet moisture but does not establish correct exhaust state, available work or heater extraction limits. For scale, the implemented bound is 424.784 kJ per kg of inlet water, or 92.026 kJ per kg of inlet mixture; the independent reversible mixture drop is 349.544 kJ/kg mixture before any efficiency factor. This comparison diagnoses a missing thermodynamic model, not a claimed generator-power error or a calibrated turbine-efficiency prediction. `tests/limits.rs:399` calls the production pure-water helper to obtain its expected bound, so it cannot detect this inconsistency.
- **fix:** Either explicitly exclude gas-bearing turbine admission while retaining bypass purging, or implement an expansion model that conserves water and air separately, resolves outlet vapour/liquid composition and common temperature, and carries the associated mixture energy/entropy consistently. Frozen gas composition is suitable only for a qualified all-vapour approximation; do not combine it with equilibrium water condensation. Add independent wet-mixture and all-vapour limits plus the existing pure-steam recovery checks.
