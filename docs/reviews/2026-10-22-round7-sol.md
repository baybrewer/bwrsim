## R1 — Frozen vapour composition contradicts turbine condensation

- **severity:** medium
- **area:** physics
- **file:line:** crates/bwr-core/src/bop.rs:589
- **wrong:** `mixture` scales both endpoint pressures by the inlet water mole fraction and delegates to an independently isentropic pure-water expansion. Gas-phase composition cannot stay fixed when that expansion condenses water. In the sealed 90 C/180 t/100 kg air fixture, negligible line loss gives total inlet/exhaust pressures 229956.453/5928.620 Pa and inlet water mole fraction 0.305198471. The code selects exhaust water pressure 1809.406 Pa and a reversible water endpoint of 15.919630 C and quality 0.849684668. With the same conserved air and water, that quality instead implies gas-phase water mole fraction 0.271791155, not 0.305198471; its assumed partial pressure and phase split contradict one another. Independent IF97 reproduces quality 0.849678967 and mole fraction 0.271789827, ruling out interpolation as the explanation. A common-temperature equilibrium expansion must solve the water phase split and total mixture entropy together. An isolated ideal-air/IF97 reversible calculation gives 8.684187 C, quality 0.526129215 and water mole fraction 0.189590774. This is a limiting thermodynamic comparison, not a calibrated plant prediction. Water equilibrium properties use the published [IAPWS-IF97 formulation](https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IF97-Rev).
- **consequence:** The inlet phase error is removed, but gas-bearing turbine moisture and available shaft work remain based on an inconsistent outlet state. The fixture returns a 424784 J/kg-water work limit from the wrong expansion. The added assertion at `crates/bwr-core/tests/limits.rs:399` calls the same water-only helper with the same frozen partial pressures, so it enforces the approximation rather than independently validating mixture expansion.
- **fix:** Either reject/isolate gas-bearing turbine admission while retaining bypass purge, or carry conserved species through a common-temperature mixture expansion that solves water condensation, vapour mole fraction and mixture entropy/enthalpy consistently. Include air thermal/work terms if supporting this domain. Replace the self-referential expansion assertion with an independent equilibrium endpoint and energy/entropy bound; retain the all-vapour inlet and pure-steam recovery checks.

## R2 — Air-rich turbine expansion silently clamps below the water-property domain

- **severity:** medium
- **area:** correctness
- **file:line:** crates/bwr-core/src/bop.rs:589
- **wrong:** The new multiplication `p_cond * y_water` can put an accepted turbine endpoint below the saturation table's minimum, with no rejection or supported extension. A consistent sealed 40 C/180 t/100 kg air vessel with open MSIVs, TCV=0.001 and the same 35 C condenser has total inlet/exhaust pressures 135304.839/5928.620 Pa and water mole fraction 0.054576221. `mixture` requests 323.561675 Pa at the water exhaust. `steam.rs:9` starts its saturation table at 1 C, and `sat_p` at `steam.rs:102` silently clamps to that first state (about 657 Pa). The inlet consequently reports `p_cond=323.561675` while its expansion endpoint properties are exactly 1 C, producing a nonzero 263722.673 J/kg-water work limit without indicating unsupported physics. This pressure is also below the ordinary liquid-vapour triple-point domain; it cannot be repaired by treating the clamped 1 C state as its saturation state.
- **consequence:** Air-rich turbine cases return a plausible numerical work limit from properties at a different pressure. The total condenser pressure is within range, so checking only that total cannot catch the newly introduced species-pressure domain violation. Existing round7 tests exercise the 90 C mixture only and miss this limit.
- **fix:** Validate species-state pressures before evaluating properties and explicitly reject/isolate unsupported gas-bearing turbine admission, or implement and qualify an appropriate mixture/low-pressure phase model. Propagate the unsupported-state result through the simulator's existing rejection path. Add the consistent 40 C air-rich fixture and a pressure/composition boundary sweep that verifies rejection or independently valid endpoint properties, rather than silent clamping.
